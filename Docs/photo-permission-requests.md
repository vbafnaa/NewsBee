# Photo requests for Wedgwood and Moses Lake

These are unsent requests based on the [researched candidate inventory](image-supply-current-story-candidates.json), checked November 3, 2026. No grant has been inferred from a public page or press folder.

In the journalist workspace, open **Review inputs → Photo supply**. Prepare an invitation, verify its exact coverage and expiry, then copy the generated request into your own correspondence. Localhost links work only on your machine; use the deployed journalist address for external recipients. Invitation links expire after at most 14 days and can be revoked independently of previously approved grants.

Ask each rights holder for original files, actual capture dates, factual captions, photographer credits, authority to grant use, any required permissions for depicted people, and no-charge editorial publication, archiving and cropping rights. The submission form records these confirmations. An administrator reviews the resulting submission before it becomes a catalog source. File resolution, visual review and story matching remain separate pipeline checks.
