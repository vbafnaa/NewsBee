Product design / v1 / 16 October 2026

Newsbee Local news, shared.

A central publisher creating trustworthy weekly community newspapers: full reporting on local websites, delivered through email previews.

MVP hypothesis: multiple communities can sustain useful weekly editions with little human intervention.

01 / Product contract

Confirmed Reader experience

  • One shared weekly edition per community.
  • Full, self-contained stories on the community website; visible source links and attribution.
  • Email is a useful preview, not the full publication.
  • No reader accounts or individual behavioral profiles.
  • Shorter editions in quiet weeks; regional material only when locally relevant and clearly labeled.

Confirmed Coverage & business

  • Local events, people, culture, civic affairs, housing, crime, and other public data.
  • Broad topic eligibility, including sensitive reporting; exceptions go to human judgment.
  • Clearly labeled ad or sponsor placements, separate from editorial stories.
  • Central operation for MVP; paid local-partner control is a future direction.
Personalization means community adaptation. Aggregate interests tune discretionary coverage for the whole location. They do not create a different newspaper for each person or displace essential news.

Outside MVP scope: individual interest profiles, sponsored editorial articles, independent-publisher operations. Source internals and technical implementation remain blackboxed; source-specific agents and orchestration are shown as logical components. Location-specific subdomains are optional, not decided.

03 / Editorial safeguards Proposed rules

RuleRequired behavior
Protect essential newsConsequential local information remains eligible regardless of engagement. Define importance explicitly; do not rely on popularity.
Evidence before fluencyAssess support for material claims, not source count or writing confidence. Distinguish facts, allegations, estimates, and interpretation.
Honest completenessNo invented quotes, experiences, interviews, or local color. Narrow, defer, escalate, or omit when evidence is insufficient.
Contextual sensitivityApply rules for privacy, identifiable people, conflicting accounts, allegations, and statistical comparisons. Sensitive topics are not blanket exclusions.
Independent exceptionsHold unresolved stories without blocking the edition. An unanswered review request never becomes approval by timeout.
Publication continuityConnect updates to prior reporting. Correct affected stories and editions visibly; send a separate notice when warranted.
Commercial separationAssess advertiser suitability and placement independently. Payment does not buy editorial inclusion or influence. An edition can publish without ads.

Gate outcomes: publish; narrow or defer; escalate; exclude. Revisions return through publication checks. Ethical exclusions are not automatically overridable by a reviewer.

04 / Community learning

Confirmed Adapt within limits

Use location-level interest signals to automatically tune discretionary coverage. Preserve the essential core. No individual reading history or account-based personalization.

Candidate signals: dwell time, topic reactions, and feedback forms.

Proposed Interpret signals carefully

Separate topic preference, usefulness, and reporting problems. Adjust gradually; account for exposure and sparse participation. Leave room for undercovered subjects and groups.

Dwell time is ambiguous. Vocal participants do not represent everyone.

Data boundary: subscriptions still require email and community choice. Keep delivery records separate from engagement measurement; define collection and retention. Reporting-problem feedback belongs with editorial oversight, not popularity scoring.

05 / Operating model

Autonomy with an exception desk

Confirmed Publish autonomously under strong rules; queue material needing judgment. Use review outcomes to reduce avoidable intervention over time.

Proposed Give every exception an owner, rationale, priority, and expiry. Audit published work as well as held stories. Evaluate rule changes before widening autonomy.

Central now, bounded delegation later

Confirmed Central publisher owns MVP operations. Future paid partners may gain defined local responsibilities.

Proposed Keep evidence, privacy, corrections, and ad separation publisher-wide. Allow local identity, regional relevance, and discretionary emphasis to vary.

Scaling constraint: missing coverage is not evidence that nothing happened. New locations need a credible coverage promise and exception capacity, not just a website.

06 / Success criteria

Primary outcome Multiple communities sustain useful weekly editions with little human intervention.

DimensionProposed evidence
ReliabilityConsecutive useful editions delivered on schedule across multiple communities.
UsefulnessLocal relevance, consequential coverage, actionable information, and reader-reported value.
Human workloadTime per community including review, corrections, policy maintenance, and advertising; exception age and backlog.
Editorial qualityMaterial errors, unsupported claims, missed escalations, and correction severity.
Expansion healthOnboarding effort, coverage gaps, and incremental workload when a community is added.

Do not use autonomy rate or clicks as the sole success metric. Numerical targets and the evaluation period are open.

07 / Open questions Not decided

Resolve these before treating v1 as a publication-ready operating policy.

What is the initial community unit?

Town, neighborhood, or county? Define boundaries, overlapping coverage, and what makes regional news locally relevant.

What qualifies as essential news?

Define protected coverage, prioritization, topic diversity, and edition-length limits.

Where does autonomous eligibility end?

Set evidence, privacy, allegation, and public-data rules; identify mandatory human-review cases and non-publishable material.

Who handles exceptions, and how much capacity exists?

Name accountable owners, turnaround expectations, expiry rules, and acceptable weekly human workload.

When does website content publish?

Weekly release only, or stories throughout the week? Define email timing and urgent-correction notices separately from breaking news.

What permits a community to launch or requires a pause?

Set coverage sufficiency and minimum usefulness standards, including the case where too little remains after stories are held.

How much can feedback change the edition?

Set signal sufficiency, adaptation limits, safeguards against repeated participation, and aggregate-data collection and retention.

What commercial boundaries apply?

Define advertiser eligibility, review requirements, placement limits, and conflict handling.

What proves the MVP works?

Choose initial communities, observation period, quality thresholds, and acceptable intervention per community per week.
